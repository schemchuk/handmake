## 2. The project in one page

"Faden und Stimmung" is a weekly handmade circle in Landshut, led by a volunteer (the **author**).
Volodymyr is the operator and developer. Three goals, in this order:
1. **Portfolio** for Volodymyr: real end-to-end automation (bot, AI steps, pipeline, CI/CD, docs).
2. **Real use and audience:** inform participants, entertain followers, grow a lead base.
3. **Resellable product:** the circle is the reference case of a platform for bloggers and groups.
Monetization (a shop with the author's works) is designed now and switched on later.

What runs today (phase 1, T00-T21): the author writes Ukrainian posts and sends photos to a private
Telegram bot (Cloudflare Worker + D1). Claude translates to German. She previews, edits and publishes.
`repository_dispatch` starts a GitHub Actions publish job that writes the post to `content/` in this
public repo, delivers it to the Telegram channel idempotently (ledger per op) and calls back the
Worker. Crons draft meeting announcements and reminders (T20). The Telegram adapter can send and
close polls (T21).

## 3. What spec v5 adds (read docs/01-spec.md, sections 1, 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 14)

- **Contest (F19, redefined):** participants hand photos of their works to the author; she uploads
  them to the bot as contest entries with a consent button and an optional first name or nickname.
  A weekly channel poll picks the winner; AI decides on no votes or a tie. Participants never use the
  system. Names are personal data and must never enter Git.
- **Website (F22-F24):** a bilingual author showcase: about, collections of her works, blog, contest
  winners, legal pages. Works and collections are new content types, created through the bot.
- **Video (F20-F21, phase 2b):** short vertical videos from a new work of the author, deterministic,
  rendered outside the Worker, published to channels with video capability.
- **Funnel (F25-F27):** anonymous weekly audience metrics now; email capture via a ready service
  with double opt-in later (needs Volodymyr's approval).
- **Monetization (F28-F29, phase 4):** a per-tenant switch and a shop for the author's works only.
- **Phases:** 1b (contest, Instagram) -> 2 (site) -> 2b (video) -> 3 (metrics, email) -> 4 (shop) ->
  P (blogger offering).

## 5. Questions for you (answer each, briefly)

   dispatch payload schema, publisher `buildPostFromPayload`, ledger, callback)? Name files.
2. **Placeholders.** Is `{entry:<id>}` in committed texts plus resolution at render (publisher) and

4. **Publish workflow.** The `site` job and later `render-video` touch `.github/workflows/`. Any risk to

   your T22 understanding survives, and how would you split the new F19 work into tasks of under ~400
   non-test lines (ADR 009)?

   phases and should be cut or deferred? Be concrete.
7. **Risks.** Anything in ADR 011-014 you consider wrong, missing or unsafe (secrets, personal data,
   cost, vendor limits)?
